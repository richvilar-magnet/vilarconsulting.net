How an engagement runs

Small first version, then tune it

  1. Rules session

    About an hour. You walk me through how you decide today, using real examples.

  2. First version

    I build it and set it running on your accounts, with a log you can check.

  3. Two weeks of tuning

    You read what it produces and tell me what's wrong. I fix it.

  4. After that

    Changes when you need them, billed by the hour.
